Step 4 of 8 The AI

Clean on paper, so where's her voice?

Then the AI drafted the scene and reviewed it before I saw a word. Its scan found 0 hard problems and 6 soft ones, and it dismissed every soft one with a reason. On paper it was clean, but the girl telling the story barely had a voice, and she kept calling her bandmate "unnie," which she never does anywhere else in the novel.

PrincipleThe AI checks its own work before I look. Voice is still my job.

What we said

Me

A demo of this scene, with a self-review, cleaned up. Check for voice accuracy, tone, atmosphere, mood. It should have a bit of that nostalgic warmth [...] but through Seoyun's younger, more joyful narrative voice.

Step 6 of 8 MeThe AI

Then 5 rounds of back and forth

The rest took about a day. The AI redrafted and reviewed itself every round, and I got more hands-on as it went, until I was selecting lines in the editor and asking for changes one at a time. Some of my catches made the rules stricter, and honestly, some pulled the other way.

PrincipleKeep what works. A rule is there to catch reflexes, and my own designs beat it.

Step 8 of 8 Me

What stuck around afterward?

The scene's published now, but the rulings outlived it. Each one went into my style guide, my scanner or its tests, so it applies to every scene I've written since, and the next scene starts a little closer to what I want.

PrincipleRulings become rules and tests, so I don't make the same correction twice.

Where 1 of my rulings ended up

My ruling, round 1

'Casual as suggesting lunch' type voice-quality similes are banned too. I'd rather just use an adverb.

The style guide

Delivery similes are out. Use an adverb.

The scanner

A delivery simile rule in rules/default.py, so the pattern gets flagged automatically from then on.

The test pair

1 line the scanner must flag, and 1 it must leave alone, so nobody widens the rule into flagging plain dialogue.

flag | delivery simile | "We should go," she said, casual as suggesting lunch.
pass | delivery simile | "We should go," she said lightly.
A memory note

Facts that aren't regex material go in a small note the AI reads every session, like Seoyun never calls Haeun unnie.
