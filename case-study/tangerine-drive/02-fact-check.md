# 02. The fact check

**Written by:** the AI did the research, 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-22. I made the calls, and I watched the key moment in the footage myself.

These characters are built from real reference footage (travel vlogs and a reality show), so the scene leans on real details: props, habits and lines people actually said. My outline guessed at several of them, and I flagged that myself: "Obviously that's very rough and needs a lot of fact checking." Each claim got checked before the plan was written.

| Claim in the outline | Verdict | What it changed |
|---|---|---|
| The house line ("Should we come build a house in the mountains...") | Confirmed: a real line, subtitled on the footage | Kept word for word. Its source show was unconfirmed at first, until I watched the scene myself. |
| Seoyun's reply is a grunt | Wrong: her real reply is "That'd be nice" | Became the ending. She never says yes, and Haeun writes it down anyway. |
| Haeun's tourist tangerine hat | Confirmed | Kept, and it got a felt leaf that flops into her eyes at every mirror check. |
| The U-turn lane story | Confirmed, with Haeun's real line, "I know, why did I?" | Kept. Seoyun does an impression of the line. |
| Tangerines are "sticky and leave a mark on you" | Close: the real line is that eating too many turns your hands yellow | The real version went in, and it's better than "sticky." |
| They've just come from picking tangerines | Wrong season: the show was filmed in March, after the field tangerines are done | The orchard moved to "last time," a November trip, and a box of hallabong (late-season citrus) went in the car instead. |
| 1 trip or several | Very likely 1 trip, with everyone off doing their own thing | Haeun's solo day on that trip became a plant: she spent it scouting this road for Seoyun. |
| "The things that bring you peace follow you everywhere" | Unconfirmed | Cut. The ocean beat says it in Seoyun's own words instead ("I know. I can still smell it, though."). |
| The overpriced flowers | Unconfirmed price | Cut. A confirmed shopping detail (a music box bill of 223,700 won) went in later. |
| The foggy mountain roads | Confirmed (the island's 2 mountain highways) | The fog became the whole look of the scene. |

The rule for using what came back: a real detail goes in as a prop or a habit, 1 clause each, and the narration never explains it. A reader who knows the footage catches it, and anyone else just gets a scene.
