# 03. The scene plan

**Written by:** the AI wrote the plan, 2026-10-21, from my outline, the fact check and a round of my notes. I set the voices ("warm, happy, a conversation between friends, not quips"), picked from its lettered options, and did the microblocking pass with it, taking every idea ("all in").

How options work: wherever a line could go several ways, the plan offers (a), (b) and (c), with the AI's recommendation first. I pick 1, combine 2, or write my own. Every pick is a small, recorded judgment about my taste, which is the raw material the whole system runs on.

## The set

March. A rental car climbing a mountain road through fog, on a supply run for the guesthouse they're staying at for a reality show. Haeun drives, in the tangerine hat she bought last November. Seoyun is in the passenger seat with her sneakers up on the dash and a hallabong from the guests' box in her lap, her phone on the aux looping a 1972 trot song.

A camera from the show sits on the dashboard with its red light on. The most private moment in the scene happens on camera, and nobody says so.

## The voices

- **Seoyun at 20:** mumbly and sweet underneath, teasing, food-brained, laughs at her own jokes. Light swearing in her head, aimed at the scene and never at Haeun, and none once the scene goes soft.
- **Haeun at 21:** soft and fond, a nagging big sister, easily set off laughing, practical talk over feelings. She says the big things lightly, in passing.
- **How they talk:** run-on and overlapping, unfinished sentences, laughing mid-line, little Korean fillers. Nobody is trying to win.
- **The prose:** warm and unhurried, long sentences, no labels. While Seoyun's eyes are shut, no sight at all.

## The 4 movements

The plan as I saw it, options included. Every "unnie" in here is the AI's mistake, and it carried straight into draft 1 (see `05-review-notes.md`).

### Movement 1: the song

Music before picture: the scene opens on one romanized line of the song.

> *Jeo pureun chowon wie geurim gateun jibeul jitgo -*
>
> "Seoddaeng-ah," Haeun said, laughing, with her eyes on the fog, "how many times are you gonna play this? It's been this song the whole way up the mountain."

- **[L0-A] Seoyun defends the song:**
  - (a) Seoyun sank lower in her seat and kept singing under her breath. "Because it's good, unnie. Just listen to it. Listen to this part -" She went for the high note and missed it by a mile, grinning the whole way up, and Haeun had to stop laughing to check her mirror. *(recommended: she answers by singing, so the song keeps playing under the scene)*

  - (c) "You liked it the first ten times."
- **The hallabong, warm:** Seoyun is peeling one from the guesthouse's box, the oil spraying when she digs her thumb in.
  > "You're gonna get that all over the seat," Haeun said.
  >
  > "It's for the guests, unnie. I'm testing it for the guests." Seoyun split it and held half out to her. "Here. Quality control. Open."
  >
  > Haeun let her feed her a piece without taking her eyes off the road, chewing, and her whole face went soft. "Okay, that's really good."

### Movement 2: the U-turn (trust)

Seoyun watches Haeun's hands at ten and two, the hat's felt leaf flopping every time she checks the mirror.

> "You're actually really good at this now," Seoyun said.
>
> "What do you mean, NOW? I've always been good at driving."
>
> "Unnie. The U-turn lane."

- **[L0-C] Haeun's comeback:**
  - (a) "Aaah, why would you bring that up -" Haeun groaned, laughing. "I KNOW, okay? Why did I even do that? I still don't know why." *(recommended: her real line, "I know, why did I?", folded into the laugh)*
  - (b) "We said we'd never talk about that. We PROMISED."
  - (c) both, in that order
- Seoyun drops her head back against the headrest, shuts her eyes and makes the instructor's face, and Haeun laughs so hard she has to slow down. "Stop it, stop, I'm gonna crash -" Haeun drives carefully, so Seoyun trusts her, and it previews the eyes-closed game.

### What I picked

- **L0-A:** none of them. I wanted the scene to open on a black frame instead: untagged dialogue in the dark, with the names in the lines telling you who's talking.
- **L0-B to L0-E:** (a) each time.
- **L0-F:** (a), until the fact check turned up Seoyun's real reply. The ending became "*Mm*." / "Is that a yes?" / "That'd be nice."

## Microblocking (I took all of it)

- **The house drawn in the fog:** Seoyun draws on the fogged passenger window with one finger, a little house with lumpy tangerine trees. The defrost later eats it in from the edges, right before Haeun offers to build a real one.
- **The seats:** Haeun's pulled all the way forward, hugging the wheel. Seoyun's tipped all the way back, sneakers on the dash.
- **The eggs:** a supply run (eggs, toilet paper, hallabong for the guests). Haeun takes every hairpin slowly "for the eggs," and they rattle on the curves.
- **The indicator for nobody:** Haeun signals every turn on an empty mountain road. With her eyes shut, that ticking is how Seoyun knows who she's riding with.
- **Drumming the trot beat** on the glovebox, *ppong*-*jjak*.
- **Feeding Haeun** hallabong without Haeun taking her eyes off the road.
- **The hallabong cap:** Seoyun sets the bumpy top on her own head to match Haeun's hat, then drops it down the side of the seat.
- **Haeun narrates her own driving** under her breath, and Seoyun whispers along to tease her, which leads into the U-turn.
- **Feet off the dash:** ignored every time, a running gag.
- **Haeun sings the line** she complained about, during the eyes-closed stretch. Her question has been coming for a while, and the reader hears it first.
- **The show camera:** Seoyun pulls a face at it, then tilts it at the fog. "None of this is going in the final cut." That's why the scene isn't in the real footage.

## The Instax (my idea, 2026-10-21)

The instant camera Haeun carries through the rest of the novel is Seoyun's, and this is the scene where Seoyun forces it on her. My message: "What if it's actually Seoyun's, and in this scene we reveal she gives it to Haeun? (Or rather, she forces it on Haeun, who declines at first, but Seoyun keeps pushing it until they both laugh and Haeun relents.)"

- Seoyun snaps Haeun at the wheel, with the flash going off in the fog, and sets the print on the dash to develop, no shaking.
- She pushes the camera on Haeun, and Haeun says no. Seoyun hooks the strap over Haeun's head while both her hands are on the wheel.
- "It's a hassle now. Later it's treasure." Seoyun says it first, and Haeun lives by it for the rest of the novel. Nobody points that out.
- Seoyun gives away the camera and keeps the one photo.
