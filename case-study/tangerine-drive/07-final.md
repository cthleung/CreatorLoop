# 07. The final scene

**Written by:** the AI drafted it, and I revised it over 5 rounds, editing lines by hand from round 4 on (see `06-revisions.md`). This is the scene as published, with every name changed.

* * * *

**TWO YEARS AGO - MARCH 2022**: **SOME MOUNTAIN ROAD, JEJU ISLAND**

**SEOYUN**

*HEYDAY Weekend House, Ep. 2*

* * * *

*I want to build a house like a painting*
*On the green grasslands*
*And live my whole life*
*With my beloved*

*We'll sow seeds in the spring*
*And in the summer flowers will grow*
*Have a good harvest in the fall*
*And be happy in the winter*

*I might want to show off with a fashionable tall building*
*I might want to live by what's trendy*
*But with my beloved, even a thatched cottage*
*Would be fine, if I was with my love*
*If I was living with my beloved*

Nam Jin, "님과 함께" (1972)

* * * *

"Seoddaeng-ah, how many times are you gonna play this?"

"Shh, Haeddeong, this is the best part -"

"Hey, you've been saying that since the bottom of the mountain -"

"Because it's GOOD! *I want to build a house like a painting, on the green grasslands* - bam bba-bba-bba-bba bam bam -"

"That's not how it goes, Seoddaeng -"

"So what? I only learned the important bits."

Seoyun opened her eyes on fog. The tires hissed on the wet road, and the whole car swayed into another bend.

She had her seat tipped back as far as it went and her sneakers up on the dash, so the only road she got was a patch of white between her knees. Haeun had gone the other way and pulled hers so far forward that her chin was practically on the steering wheel, squinting into the white with both hands locked at the top. On the aux, Nam Jin started over from the beginning, and Seoyun drummed along on the glovebox, *ppong*-*jjak*, *ppong*-*jjak*. Trot was a rhythm game for grandpas, and she was full-comboing it.

Haeun turned Nam Jin down to nothing whenever the GPS lady opened her mouth, because apparently Kang Haeun would die if she missed a single turn, and Seoyun cranked him back up the second the lady shut up. They'd been at it since the guesthouse, and Seoyun was winning.

The Weekend House crew had stuck a camera to the windshield back at the guesthouse, and its red light blinked at Seoyun, waiting for content. She gave it the Fish Market Ajumma face, one eye squeezed shut, the other eyebrow up and her mouth screwed off to one side, then reached up and tilted it at the side window and a wall of white.

"None of this is going in the final cut," she told it.

"YAH, PD-nim's gonna kill you," Haeun laughed.

"Don't care, he can come up here and fight me then."

Every few bends the fog tore open on a canola field so yellow it looked photoshopped, then shut again. Seoyun sat up for the next one.

"Holy shit, it's like a painting -"

"Picture! Seoddaeng, get a picture -"

Seoyun was still patting around for her camera when the white rolled back over it.

"Ugh, some weather fairy," Seoyun grumbled, rolling her eyes.

"I've retired, take it up with the clouds."

"Turn right ahead," said the GPS lady, and Nam Jin vanished. Seoyun gave Haeun the turn out of the goodness of her heart, then cranked him back up.

The eggs were the reason they were up here at all. They were the cooking team's problem, which made them Seoyun's problem, and the toilet paper wedged in next to them was hospitality's, which made it Haeun's. The box of hallabong was for the guests coming tomorrow, so until then it was up for grabs. Haeun was taking every hairpin at a crawl for the sake of some fucking eggs.

"A tractor's gonna pass us, *halmeoni*."

"You have to go slow with stuff that breaks," Haeun declared. "You know what I mean?"

Seoyun twisted round to the back seat. "Hear that, eggs? She thinks you're weak. This isn't your limit." She turned back round. "Hey, you're driving like that's LUMEN's juice back there."

Haeun gave the windshield a tiny bow over the wheel. "I didn't even carry it, Jiwon did. I just had MANNERS."

"And Wonji introduced herself to their carpet, remember?" Seoyun added with a smirk.

Haeun snorted so hard the felt leaf on her hat jiggled.

By then Seoyun had the hallabong box open. She dug her thumb in under the bumpy top of one, and the peel sprayed oil across the dash. Somebody had to check these weren't poison before the guests got them, and the cooking team took that shit seriously.

"Quality control," she said, splitting it. "Open up."

Haeun opened her mouth with her eyes on the road, and Seoyun fed her a chunk. Haeun chewed and slumped back against her seat with a happy little groan. "Okay, the guests can't have these. These are ours now."

"Don't get comfortable. You're still banned from the kitchen."

"I made rice ONE time -"

"It was crunchy AND soggy, Haeddeong. That's not even possible."

Seoyun balanced the knobbly cap of peel on her own head to match Haeun's hat. It rolled off at the next bend and dropped down the side of the seat, and she spent the rest of the verse digging for it.

Haeun had bought the hat the last time they were here, back in November, a knitted tangerine with the felt leaf sprouting out of the top, and the bitch had worn it every day of this trip, which Seoyun respected. It sat high enough that her ears stuck out under it. Every mirror check knocked the leaf into her eyes, and when she shoved it back, Seoyun caught a yellow canola hairpin clipped underneath. Haeun had been buying it for Jiwon and Nari right up until she kept it. Something about flowers reminding her of Seoyun. Gross.

"You ditched us last time," Seoyun said, and flicked a bit of peel at her. "Went off on your own little day. Was it everything you dreamed of?"

Haeun smiled at the fog. "It was really nice, actually. I'd always wanted to do that, just go somewhere by myself and -"

Seoyun had spent her own day in a tangerine orchard with a bug, which had also been really fucking nice, thanks. She reached over and turned the song up.

Haeun laughed and turned it back down, then kept her hand on the knob like a bouncer. "You went off by yourself too, tangerine ghost."

"That's different. I had the tangerines."

The heater only reached the middle of the windshield, and the side windows had fogged up at the edges. While the song started over, Seoyun drew on her side of the glass with a fingertip, a house with a pointy roof and tangerine trees all round it. The trees came out lumpy.

Haeun glanced over. "Is that a house?"

"It's a potato farm."

Seoyun's Instax, the sky-blue one she took everywhere, was rattling around in the cupholder under Haeun's bag of dried squid. She dug it out and caught Haeun in the viewfinder, hat on and both fists round the wheel. The flash bounced off the glass, and Haeun yelped.

"Yah, not now, I look like a preschooler -"

The print slid out with a whir, and Seoyun laid it face up on the dash. People who shook them were animals, and the fun was waiting to see how it came out. Then she held the camera out across the console.

"Here, keep it. Take your own canola pictures."

Haeun's eyes flicked to the camera and back to the road. "What? It's YOURS."

"You got me a music box. You nearly blew 223,700 won on us in one shop. *Michin-naen*, take the camera."

"Did you just call me a crazy bi- YAH, not everyone from Gwangju TALKS like that! And we're on CAMERA!"

"Then PD-nim can bleep it, take the camera."

"A music box doesn't need film, Seoddaeng. I'll have to buy film. I'll have to carry it around EVERYWHERE -"

"It's a hassle now," Seoyun said. "Later it's treasure. Write that down."

Haeun opened her mouth to argue, so Seoyun hooked the strap over her head while her hands were busy on the wheel. Haeun shrieked. "Yah, I'm DRIVING -" The camera thumped against her chest and knocked the hat down over her eye, and the two of them laughed until Haeun gave up and let it hang.

"Congratulations," Seoyun said. "Ddaeng Ddong has an official photographer."

Haeun jerked her chin up to see out from under the hat. "And where am I supposed to put all the pictures?"

"On the fridge, obviously. You'll thank me."

At the next junction Haeun stopped to let the fog go first and made a grab for the print on the dash. "Seoddaeng-ah, let me see, I wanna know how bad it is -"

Seoyun slapped her hand away. "It's still white."

"So shake it."

"Say that again and there's no pajeon for you tonight." She set the print back face up. "Memories take time to develop, okay? You can't rush it."

"Okay, *halabeoji*."

Past the junction Haeun was back to narrating her driving under her breath. "Okay, left. Okay. Slow, slow..." Seoyun whispered along a beat behind her until Haeun swatted her knee.

"You've actually gotten good at this," Seoyun said.

Haeun lifted her chin at the windshield. "What are you talking about? I'm clearly the best driver in Mirae."

"Haeddeong, the U-turn lane. Remember?"

"*Aaah*, why would you bring that up -"

Seoyun did her best Haeun, wide-eyed and baffled. "*I know, why did I?*"

Haeun's ears went pink where they stuck out under the hat. Seoyun let her head drop back against the headrest and shut her eyes, just like the instructor had. She'd had the best seat for that one, in the back, losing her shit until her seatbelt locked.

Haeun laughed so hard she had to slow down. "Stop it, stop, I'm gonna crash -" Her hand jumped off the wheel to slap at Seoyun's ankles, and the curve yanked it back. "Feet off the dash, I can't see the mirror."

Seoyun opened one eye and crossed her ankles on the dash.

"Okay, okay," Haeun said. "Close your eyes."

"Is this how you get rid of me? Push me out on a hairpin?"

Haeun was biting down on a smile. "The eggs would never forgive me. Close them, just play with me, okay?"

Seoyun closed them. The car leaned into a bend with the eggs rattling behind her, and the indicator started ticking. Haeun signaled every turn for the benefit of the fog, and in the dark that ticking was the Haeddeong she knew.

The GPS lady piped up about a bend, and Nam Jin ducked out for her and came straight back. Seoyun's hands were in her lap.

"What do you smell -"

"*Hallabong* tangerines," Seoyun answered immediately.

"You're HOLDING one, you cheater." A knuckle jabbed her arm.

"It's in the car, it counts."

"You're gonna turn into one, you know?"

Seoyun grinned with her eyes still shut. "Fine by me. They remind me of you."

"Why, because of my hat?"

"No... it's like, if you eat too many, your hands go all yellow, right? And it doesn't come off for days." Seoyun laughed. "That's so you."

The wipers squeaked across the glass and back, and the song started over. Then Haeun was singing under her breath, and it was the farming verse that Seoyun bam-bba-bammed through every single time.

"*We'll sow seeds in the spring, and in the summer flowers will grow, have a good harvest in the fall, and be happy in the winter -*"

Haeun had been whining about this song since the bottom of the mountain, and yet she knew all the lyrics, seeds and all. So apparently the farming verse was important too. Seoyun kept her eyes shut and let her sing.

"Your turn," Seoyun said. "Eyes shut."

"Seoddaeng-ah, somebody has to look at the road."

"I'll look at it for you, I promise -"

"Your eyes are CLOSED," Haeun cracked up, and the car lurched one way with the eggs rattling and then the other.

"Careful," Seoyun said, eyes still shut. "Stuff that breaks."

"If I close mine, we both go off this mountain, and then who sings our parts? Fine, what else do you smell?"

Seoyun pulled in a long breath through her nose. Hallabong, obviously, it was all over her fingers, and Haeun's squid, ew, why the hell did she even buy that, and then the sea, which was stupid.

"...The ocean."

"Seoddaeng, we're on a MOUNTAIN."

"I know. I can still smell it, though." Maybe it'd been stuck in her hair since last November.

The fan roared up, and warm air poured past Seoyun's face. Then Nam Jin dropped out again, even though the GPS lady had been keeping her trap shut.

"You know where I went last time? On my day by myself?" Haeun said. "I drove up here. This road. And the whole time I kept seeing stuff and thinking, Seoddaeng would love this."

"Then you should've taken a picture, *babo*."

The camera thumped as Haeun patted it. "So now I can."

Seoyun peeked out of one eye. Haeun flashed her a grin, and Seoyun's ears went hot, and it wasn't the heater's fault. She shut the eye again.

"When we move out, we're getting the biggest fridge in Seoul," she said. "To put them all up."

The song got round to the last verse. This one Seoyun actually knew, and right on cue, Nam Jin came back up past anything Seoyun had gotten away with all morning. Haeun had cranked him herself. She practically belted it out, being the karaoke showoff that she was, while Seoyun mumbled along into the window.

"*But with my beloved, even a thatched cottage would be fine -*"

And when the song ran out, Haeun cut in before it could start over. "Say, Seoddaeng-ah."

"Hm?"

"I was thinking. Should we come build a house in the mountains at Jeju-do and live here later?" A wiper squeaked across the glass. "We could grow our own tangerines and stuff. Have everyone over and make them eat our goodies till they're sick of them."

Seoyun opened her eyes. The defrost had eaten her potato farm in from the edges until only the roof was left, and past it the fog had thinned to black stone walls going by. Against the black, the window turned into a mirror.

Her own face was in it, and over her shoulder was Haeun's, eyes on the road, with the hat slipping and the camera strap across her chest. The little roof sat right over both their heads. The tips of Haeun's ears had gone pink. Up on the windshield, the crew camera was still pointed at the fog, red light on.

The print on the dash had finished developing. It was Haeun at the wheel in the hat, mouth open mid-yelp and blown out by the flash. Seoyun slid it into her hoodie pocket and pressed her forehead to the cold glass beside what was left of the house.

"*Mm*," Seoyun murmured.

"Is that a yes?"

Seoyun kept her forehead on the glass. "That'd be nice."

Haeun giggled. "Okay, I'm writing that down."

Seoyun shut her eyes again, smiling into the window. Tangerines, and the ocean, and that black rock, and the ocean again, and then tangerines, going round with the song, round again.

Tangerines. Ocean. Rock. Ocean. Tangerines.
