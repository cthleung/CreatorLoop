## The beats

The AI's summary of the outline, which became the spine of the scene plan:

1. Haeun, driving: "You've been playing that song on repeat, Seoddaeng." Seoyun deflects, and they go back and forth. They've just come from picking tangerines, and Haeun notes how much Seoyun loved it.
2. Seoyun at the window. "You've gotten better at this" (the driving). "Remember the U-turn lane?" Haeun: we don't talk about that, and laughs.
3. Haeun asks Seoyun to close her eyes and play a game: what does she smell? Tangerines. Haeun, in her tangerine hat, teases that she's obsessed. "They remind me of you." "Because they're orange?" Then the real answer. Haeun goes quiet.
4. Seoyun tells Haeun to play. Haeun can't, she's driving, and they bicker.
5. "What else?" The ocean, though they're on a mountain. Then Haeun's line: should we come build a house in the mountains at Jeju-do and live here later? Seoyun grunts with her head on the glass, her version of yes.
6. Fade out on her drifting thoughts (tangerines, ocean, rock, ocean, tangerines), then fade into the present with the same words mirrored, then the sound fades in.
