# Gold cases for tools/rule_engine.py and rules/default.py. tests/test_rule_engine.py runs every line.
#
#   flag | <rule label> | <line>    the engine MUST put this label on the line
#   pass | <rule label> | <line>    the engine must NOT put this label on the line
#   pass | *            | <line>    the engine must put NO label at all on the line
#
# Every ruling on a rule becomes a pair: the line it should catch, and the line it should leave alone.
# The pass line is the important one, since it stops the next regex change from creeping into ground
# that's already been ruled fine.

# ---- house
flag | em dash | She waited — then left.
pass | em dash | She waited - then left.
flag | half (any use) | She waited half an hour.
pass | half (any use) | She waited thirty minutes.

# ---- invented numbers
flag | 4/7: counted or invented? | She waited seventeen minutes.
flag | 4/7: counted or invented? | He'd taken 4,000 pictures.
pass | 4/7: counted or invented? | She waited twenty minutes.
flag | invented count | Haeun laughed so hard she had to check her mirror twice.
pass | invented count | "I've told you twice already," Haeun said.

# ---- hedges: a reflex in narration, voice in dialogue
flag | hedge | She seemed tired.
flag | hedge | He was kind of angry.
pass | hedge | "It's kinda making a really good point," she said.
pass | hedge | "Okay, that's sort of genius," Seoyun said.

# ---- narrated non-events (from the case study's draft 1)
flag | non-event | She kept singing under her breath instead of answering.
flag | non-event | Haeun opened her mouth without taking her eyes off the road.
flag | non-event | Seoyun's feet stayed where they were.
pass | non-event | Seoyun opened one eye and crossed her ankles on the dash.

# ---- delivery similes
flag | delivery simile | "We should go," she said, casual as suggesting lunch.
flag | delivery simile | "Here," he said, like he was ordering coffee.
pass | delivery simile | "We should go," she said lightly.

# ---- narrator distance
flag | filtering | She could feel the car lean into the bend.
pass | filtering | The car leaned into the bend.
flag | cognitive shortcut | She clocked the camera on the dash.
pass | cognitive shortcut | The camera's red light blinked at her.
flag | language about language | Her voice trailed off.
pass | language about language | "I'd always wanted to do that, just go somewhere by myself and -"

# ---- signposting
flag | something hook | Something shifted between them.
pass | something hook | "Something about flowers reminding her of me," Seoyun said.
flag | stopwatch | For a moment, neither of them spoke.
pass | stopwatch | "Give me a second," Haeun said.

# ---- precision emphasis
flag | precision emphasis | It was exactly the face the instructor had made.
pass | precision emphasis | "That's exactly what I said!"

# ---- AI register
flag | AI register | The fog was a tapestry of gray.
pass | * | The fog tore open on a canola field, then shut again.
